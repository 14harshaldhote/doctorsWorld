# Doctors World privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This policy covers Doctors World on iPhone and iPad.

## The short version

Doctors World collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends anything over the internet. What the game knows stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Your doctor's name and look, your progress through the story, your choices, scores and records, and your settings. The game keeps them in its own storage on your device so you can carry on where you left off.

If your device is backed up (to iCloud or to a computer), the save goes with the backup, under your own account. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

## Voices, microphone and camera

Every voice you hear was recorded for the game and comes inside it. If a line has no recording, your device's own built-in voice may read it aloud, on the device. The game never uses the microphone, the camera, your contacts, your photos or your location.

## Reminders

Reminders are off until you say yes. If you turn them on, the game works out what to say from your progress (for example, which patient is waiting) and asks your device to show it later. Your device's own notification centre does the rest. Nothing goes to a server, and nobody else can see or send them.

You can turn them off in the game's Settings, or for Doctors World in your device's Settings app.

## Nobody else

The game contains no advertising, analytics or social media code from other companies, and it shares nothing with anyone. It has nothing to share.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

## If this changes

If the game ever needs to handle your information differently, this policy will change first, with a new date at the top.

## Questions

Ask on the game's page: github.com/14harshaldhote/doctorsWorld/issues
